Trust & safety

Verification happens before anyone meets — not after something goes wrong.

Once Smile ID identity checks, proof of address, and (for babysitters) the reference affidavit are all confirmed, a profile carries a Verified Identity badge visible to the other party — so you always know who you're meeting before you meet them.

Smile ID identity verification

Facial recognition and government ID checks are mandatory for both babysitters and families — including passport and work-permit checks for freelance babysitters — before any payment or platform booking.

Paystack Split Payment

Families pay via Paystack before booking; Paystack holds the payment and only releases the babysitter's share once the booking is confirmed complete — Elite Traders Lounge never takes custody of the funds.

Short pre-booking interview

Before the first paid booking, families and sitters meet — in person or by video — to align on experience, rates, and house rules.

POPIA-compliant data handling

Personal data is processed only for booking, payment, dispute, and safety purposes — never sold or shared beyond legally required parties.

Proof of address for both parties

Every babysitter and family confirms their address with a recent utility bill, bank statement, lease, or affidavit before a placement is finalised.

Reference & affidavit checks

Every babysitter names a reference who signs a sworn Reference Affidavit confirming their character and experience — verified directly by our team.

Passport & work-permit freelancers welcome

Foreign nationals may register as freelance babysitters with a valid passport and work permit, so Elite Traders Lounge stays fully compliant with South African immigration law.

3.2 Minimum wage compliance & Appendix C

Rate bands that never fall below minimum wage.

Elite Traders Lounge uses experience-based rate bands (Level 1–4), set out in Appendix C: Rate Bands & Minimum Wage Check, and every booking must fall within these ranges. The agreed hourly rate must sit within the correct level band and must never be lower than the current National Minimum Wage — R30.23 per hour, effective 1 March 2026 under Government Gazette No. 54075 (2026/2027 rate; reviewed annually), or the applicable rate at the time of service. If a rate is entered below the platform's minimum for that level or below the legal minimum wage, that rate is void and the minimum compliant rate in Appendix C automatically applies. The family and babysitter must select the appropriate level and agree on a rate within that band before booking confirmation.
