How it works

One wallet, one booking flow, two protected parties.

Every booking follows the same post-service payment model, whether you're viewing it as a family funding a booking or a babysitter accepting one.

01

Fund your wallet

Verify with SmileID, then load funds by card, EFT, or instant EFT through the FNB-integrated gateway.

02

Book your sitter

Set date, time, duration, and rate. Funds are held, not charged — the babysitter can accept or decline.

03

Care is delivered

Your sitter follows the written care instructions you provide — feeding, naps, medication, emergency contacts.

04

Confirm within 24 hours

Confirming service completion releases payment from your wallet hold. No confirmation, no release — contact support if needed.

Trust & safety

Verification happens before anyone meets — not after something goes wrong.

SmileID identity verification

Facial recognition and government ID checks are mandatory for both babysitters and families before wallet access or platform bookings.

Escrow-style wallet holds

Families fund the wallet before booking; funds are held, not paid out, until the sitter confirms and the family verifies satisfaction.

Short pre-booking interview

Before the first paid booking, families and sitters meet — in person or by video — to align on experience, rates, and house rules.

POPIA-compliant data handling

Personal data is processed only for booking, payment, dispute, and safety purposes — never sold or shared beyond legally required parties.

Rates & commission

Rate bands that never fall below minimum wage.

Every booking must sit inside the matching experience level's band, and never below South Africa's national minimum wage (R29.58/hour, 2025 rate — reviewed annually). Rates below the floor are automatically corrected to the compliant minimum.

All payments run through the Elite Traders Lounge wallet. Off-platform cash payments fall outside the contract and outside our protection.

Zero withdrawal fees

Every booking is separate
