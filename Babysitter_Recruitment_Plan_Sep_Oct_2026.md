# Elite Traders Lounge — Babysitter Recruitment Plan
**Phase 1: September – October 2026 (8 weeks) · Sitters only, no public website launch**

## 1. Goal for this phase

Register and verify as many quality babysitters as possible across priority towns — without publicly promoting the full website or the family-facing booking side yet. Every ad, flyer, and post in this phase points to **one single destination**: the "Become a sitter" registration page. At the end of October, you'll review how many sitters are verified and in which towns, then decide where to open bookings to families next.

## 2. The one link to use everywhere

Use this link in every ad, WhatsApp message, and flyer:

`https://elite-traders-lounge.vercel.app/register-sitter.html`

One thing to know: that page still has a menu at the top (Rates, Policies, Book a sitter, etc.), so a curious visitor *could* click into the rest of the site if they look for it. That's a minor risk, not a big one — most people who land on a registration page just fill it in. If you want a completely locked-down page later that hides everything except the registration form, tell me and I can build a simple recruitment-only landing page in a few minutes.

For printed flyers, a long web address is hard to read — I'd recommend shortening it with a free tool like Bitly once you're ready to print, so it reads as something like `bit.ly/ETL-sitter`.

## 3. What happens after someone clicks (so your ad copy sets the right expectation)

1. They fill in personal details, experience level, availability, and a reference.
2. They take a selfie for identity verification and upload ID/police clearance documents (Smile ID checks this automatically).
3. They pay a **R99 annual verification fee** via a Paystack payment link (this funds the annual police clearance + Child Protection Register check).
4. They electronically sign the Babysitter Service Agreement.
5. You review and approve them on the admin dashboard — only then do they appear as "verified" and become bookable.

Worth mentioning in ads: sitters keep 80–90% of every booking fee (commission is 20% total, split by experience level), and rates start at R45/hour.

## 4. Recruitment channels (no cost or low cost)

| Channel | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Facebook Marketplace "Jobs" & local buy/sell groups | Post in town-specific groups (e.g. "Midrand Community Board", "Nelspruit Buy & Sell") — these groups are usually free and hyper-local |
| WhatsApp community/church/school groups | Ask contacts to forward your registration link in parent/school WhatsApp groups — parents often know reliable sitters looking for formal work |
| Gumtree "Childcare Jobs Offered" | Free classified postings, searchable by town |
| Flyers at crèches, clinics, churches, community halls, laundromats, taxi ranks | Physical reach for people without much data/social media |
| Local domestic worker & au pair agencies | Many already have vetted candidates looking for extra formal work — offer to co-recruit |
| Referral incentive | Offer an existing or newly-registered sitter a small once-off reward (e.g. R50–R100 airtime/EFT) for referring a friend who successfully completes verification — word of mouth is usually your strongest channel for this kind of work |
| Community radio & noticeboards (tourist towns) | In towns with a strong tourism/hospitality economy, community radio stations and lodge/guesthouse noticeboards reach locals looking for extra income |

## 8. Tracking progress

Your existing admin dashboard (`elite-traders-lounge.vercel.app/admin.html`) already lists every registered sitter with their town and verification status. Each week during this campaign:

1. Open the admin dashboard and filter/scan by town.
2. Count: new registrations this week, how many completed ID verification, how many paid the R99 fee and are fully "verified."
3. Note which towns are silent — that tells you where to push harder or change channel (e.g. more flyers, less Facebook).

A simple weekly tally in a spreadsheet (town → registered → verified) is enough — no extra tooling needed for this phase.

## 9. Reevaluating at the end of October

At the end of the 8 weeks, look at:

- **Total verified sitters** and which towns actually produced them (adverts don't always convert where you expect)
- **Coverage gaps** — towns with zero sitters, especially ones near where you expect early family demand
- **Cost/effort per verified sitter** by channel — keep doing what worked, drop what didn't

Once you have a real spread of verified sitters, that's your signal to start the next phase: opening the family-facing side of the platform in the towns where you now have coverage.
