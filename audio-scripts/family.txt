This part is for families who want to book a babysitter. Make sure you already have your free Paystack account from step one.

Step one, open the booking form. Tap the button on this page to go to the family sign up page.

Step two, fill in your details. Your full name, your I D or passport number, and your cellphone number. Your home address. This is where the babysitter will come. How many children you have, and their ages. And if you have pets, say what type.

Step three, upload two documents. A copy of your I D or passport. And a proof of address, like a light bill, from the last three months.

Step four, type in your Paystack email. Use the same email you used when you made your Paystack account in step one.

Step five, choose your booking. Pick the date, the time, and how many hours you need. You can also choose a babysitter you like, or let our team pick one for you.

Step six, take a quick selfie. This just proves it is really you. It only takes a few seconds.

Step seven, pay the ninety nine rand fee. This fee checks your identity and keeps everyone safe. Tap the Paystack pay button on the form to pay it. You only pay this once a year.

Step eight, submit the form and wait. Our team checks your details, usually within a day or two. Once approved, your babysitter booking is confirmed. Well done, you are all set.
