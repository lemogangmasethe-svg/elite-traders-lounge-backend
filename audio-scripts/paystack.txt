Hello. This is the Elite Traders Lounge getting started guide. Step one is for everyone. First, you need a free Paystack account. Paystack is the safe app that moves money between families and babysitters. Elite Traders Lounge never touches your money. Paystack does. So your money is always safe.

Here is how to make your account. First, open the Paystack sign up page. You can find the button on this page. It opens in a new tab, so you will not lose this guide.

Second, type in your details. Your full name. Your email address. Use one you check often. And a password you will remember.

Third, check your email. Paystack sends you a message to make sure it is really you. Open that email, and tap the confirm link inside it.

Fourth, add your bank account. Inside Paystack, go to Settings, and add your South African bank account. This is where your money will be paid to, or paid from.

Fifth, write down or remember the email address you used for Paystack. You will type this same email into the Elite Traders Lounge form in the next step.

That is it. Once your Paystack account is ready, go back to this page and choose whether you want to book a babysitter, or become a babysitter.
