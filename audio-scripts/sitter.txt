This part is for people who want to become a babysitter. Make sure you already have your free Paystack account from step one.

Step one, open the babysitter sign up form. Tap the button on this page to go to the sign up page.

Step two, fill in your details. Your full name, your I D or passport number, and your cellphone number. Your nationality and home address. Your years of childcare experience, and when you are available.

Step three, upload your documents. You will need clear photos or scans of your I D or passport. Proof of address, like a light bill, from the last three months. An A F I S check or police clearance certificate. A Child Protection Register clearance letter. And if you are not a South African citizen, a police clearance from your home country too. If you do not have these documents yet, the form tells you exactly how and where to apply for each one.

Step four, add a reference. Give the name and contact details of someone who knows your childcare experience. They will be contacted to confirm.

Step five, type in your Paystack email. Use the same email you used when you made your Paystack account in step one. This is where your pay will be sent.

Step six, take a selfie and a short video. This proves you are a real person, not a photo. It only takes a few seconds, and it is never recorded.

Step seven, pay the ninety nine rand fee. This fee checks your identity and documents. Tap the Paystack pay button on the form to pay it. You pay this once a year, when you renew your documents.

Step eight, submit and wait for approval. Our team checks your documents, usually within a few days. Once approved, families can find and book you.

Step nine, remember to renew every year. Once a year, you will upload fresh copies of your police clearance and Child Protection Register letter, and pay the ninety nine rand fee again. You do this from your own babysitter dashboard. We will remind you when it is due. Well done, you are all set.
