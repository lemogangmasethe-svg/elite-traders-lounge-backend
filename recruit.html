Babysitters wanted

Earn money caring for children — no experience required to start.

Whether you're a student looking to earn extra money in your spare time, or you've spent years caring for children and have the patience and love to prove it — Elite Traders Lounge has a place for you. Register below, get verified, and start receiving paid bookings from families near you.

Students & anyone new to babysitting

No formal experience yet? Register as Level 1 (Entry). You'll still be fairly paid — R45/hour minimum — while you build your track record and references.

Experienced caregivers

Years of childcare experience, First Aid/CPR training, or overnight/special-needs experience earns you a higher rate band — up to R85/hour.

Anyone with patience & love for children

What matters most is that you're reliable, trustworthy, and genuinely good with kids. We verify your identity and background so families can trust you from day one.

Every applicant completes identity verification (Smile ID) before receiving bookings, and every booking pays at or above the National Minimum Wage (R30.23/hour, effective 1 March 2026) — never negotiated down. A valid SAPS Police Clearance Certificate · JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 6MB. Don't have one yet? You can apply at any police station for a once-off fee of R190 — take your ID/passport, get SAPS 91(a) fingerprints taken, and pay at the station or by EFT; see the official SAPS guidance. It typically takes a few weeks to be issued, so we recommend applying early. This is not a once-off check — to remain verified, you'll need to submit a fresh clearance certificate every 12 months.
Proof you are not listed on Part B of South Africa's National Child Protection Register, which bars unsuitable people from working with children · JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 6MB. Apply for a free Form 30 individual enquiry by emailing your ID copy to CPRenquiries@dsd.gov.za (Department of Social Development) — it typically takes a few weeks, so apply early. Like the police clearance above, this must be renewed every 12 months to keep your verified status.
South African immigration law requires foreign nationals to also provide a police clearance certificate from any country they lived in for 12+ months as an adult during the last 5 years, issued within the last 6 months · JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 6MB. Contact that country's police or diplomatic mission for the correct process. This also needs renewing every 12 months.
Annual registration & verification fee · R99
An R99 fee covers your identity and document verification. It is completely separate from booking commission, and is the same R99 fee families pay. Because your police clearance and Child Protection Register checks must be renewed every 12 months to keep your safety verification current, this fee is charged annually rather than once — you'll pay it again each year alongside your renewal documents, from your sitter dashboard.
